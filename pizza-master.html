HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Drag across the pizza to make a cut
SERVE when your slice count matches the order
UNDO removes your last cut
RESET clears all cuts
? button pauses the timer!

HOW TO GET EACH SLICE COUNT

x21 straight cut all the way across
x32 parallel cuts that don't cross each other
x42 cuts that cross inside the pizza (like + or X)
x53 parallel cuts, or 1 crossing pair + 1 separate cut
x63 cuts each crossing the others inside (3 interior meets)
x74 cuts: 3 parallel + 1 that crosses all three
x84 cuts with 3 pairs crossing inside (like a star)
x94 cuts with 4 interior intersection points
x105 cuts with 4 interior intersections — star pattern!

SCORING

Perfect = 100 x level + time bonus
Off by 1 = half points + lose a life
Wrong = lose a life, no points
Streak bonus = +20 per consecutive perfect
Higher levels give you MORE time
